Good to know

Questions, answered.

What is included in my monthly rental?+

Your monthly fee covers the furniture, professional cleaning, delivery, assembly and collection within our standard service area.

Can I change my furniture during the rental?+

Yes. You can request a swap at any time. A small logistics fee may apply depending on your plan and location.

What happens if an item is damaged?+

Normal wear is expected. For accidental or significant damage, contact us and we will work out a fair repair or replacement solution.

Where do you deliver?+

Our initial service area is Kuala Lumpur and the Klang Valley. Contact us for larger projects elsewhere in Malaysia.
