Good to understand

Rental details, clearly explained.

Where do you deliver?+

Our standard service area covers Kuala Lumpur and the Klang Valley. For projects elsewhere in Malaysia, contact us with your location and furniture requirements for a tailored quotation.

When will my furniture arrive?+

Delivery timing depends on stock availability, order size and site access. We confirm an agreed delivery window after reviewing your request and location.

What counts as normal wear?+

Minor signs from ordinary office use are expected. Significant stains, breakage, loss or damage caused by misuse may require a fair repair or replacement charge.

Can I extend or swap furniture?+

Yes. Extensions and swaps can be arranged subject to availability. Pricing or a logistics fee may change depending on the new term, item and delivery requirements.

What happens if I return early?+

Your selected rental period is a commitment. An early return may require settlement of the remaining term. We explain the applicable amount before arranging collection.

Do you handle larger business projects?+

Yes. For larger teams, we can help with furniture selection, quantities, workspace planning, phased delivery and consolidated monthly invoicing.
